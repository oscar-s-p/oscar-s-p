I took part in the course "Life & Meaning in the Cosmos" at the University of San Francisco, develped in collaboration with the Canary Global Foundation ([more information here](https://canaryglobal.org/launching-life-meaning-in-the-cosmos-program/)).

Within this interdisciplinary initiative I taught two sessions focused on exoplanets, going from what they are and what do we know about them, to hands-on analysis of real observation data of exoplanetary transits. A repository with an exercise I developed for that code is available [here](https://github.com/oscar-s-p/exoplanets-class).
