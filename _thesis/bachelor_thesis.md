We created a numerical code to perform one-dimensional magnetohydrodynamic simulations of semi-relativistic shocks.

Under the supervision of Prof. Fernando Moreno-Insertis I developed from scratch a Python numerical code to perform simulations, starting from the ideal fluid equations and working our way up to magnetized fluids in a semi-relativistic regime. I learned the complexities of numerical codes, the importance of benchmark tests, and the elegance of simple ideas to prove complex phenomena. 

We evaluated a method used by some authors to speed calculations by artificially reducing the speed of light and we concluded that it leads to altered simulation results when strong shocks are involved.
