Hi! Here is a little bit of extra information about myself and the things I enjoy about this life apart from research!

 I was born and raised in Tenerife, the biggest of the Canary Islands. I believe it has an irresistible charm, both because of its natural landscapes (beaches and mountains) and its climate, as well as its people. Being a common characteristic shared by all the islands of the archipelago, each one is unique in its own way.
